What to check before you buy

  • Warranty: grey-market new watches usually carry the dealer's warranty, not the brand's. Ask which one you get and whether the brand's warranty card is included.
  • Exact reference: dial colour, size and strap change the price. Match the full ref (T1374271104100) before comparing deals.

History

Tissot was founded in Le Locle, Switzerland, in 1853 by Charles-Félicien Tissot and his son Charles-Émile.

Tissot released the first PRX in 1978 and re-released the integrated-bracelet PRX in 2021.

The PRX Automatic Chronograph T137.427.11.041.00 is 42 mm and 14.5 mm thick with the Valjoux A05.H31, per Jomashop.
