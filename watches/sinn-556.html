History

Sinn is a German watch manufacturer based in Frankfurt am Main, founded in 1961 by flight instructor and pilot Helmut Sinn.

The 556 I is a 38.5 mm steel watch with a black dial, SW200-1 automatic and 200 m water resistance, per WatchBuys.
