History

Rolex introduced the Explorer II in 1971 with a fixed 24-hour bezel and an independent 24-hour hand to help cave explorers tell day from night.

The current Explorer II, ref. 226570, is a 42 mm Oystersteel watch with a date, an orange 24-hour hand and a fixed 24-hour bezel, per Rolex.
