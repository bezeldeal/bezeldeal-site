What to check before you buy

  • Exact reference: dial colour, size and strap change the price. Match the full ref (52508) before comparing deals.
  • Fakes and red flags: be wary of prices far below the ranges above, stock photos only, or sellers who won't show the caseback, serial and papers.

Alternatives worth a look

  • Rolex Day-Date 40: Rolex's gold President. (40 mm 18kt Yellow Gold, Rolex Calibre 3255, retail $50,400 (m228238-0042, per Rolex))
  • Patek Philippe Aquanaut: High-end alternative. (40.8 mm stainless steel, Calibre 26-330 S C, retail $27,257 (5167A-001))
