What to check before you buy

  • Warranty: grey-market new watches usually carry the dealer's warranty, not the brand's. Ask which one you get and whether the brand's warranty card is included.
  • Exact reference: dial colour, size and strap change the price. Match the full ref (IW329303) before comparing deals.

History

IWC was founded in Schaffhausen in 1868 by American watchmaker Florentine Ariosto Jones and has belonged to Richemont since 2000.

IWC's 1940 Big Pilot's Watch was a pioneering timepiece; in 2002 IWC brought back its large case and clear dial.

The Big Pilot 43 IW329303 checked here has a blue dial and an automatic movement, per Jomashop.
