Which one I'd pick

134300 vs 124300: the new 134300 is the one to get at retail if an Official Rolex Jeweler has one. The 124300 is the same idea on the pre-owned market, but on today's numbers it isn't cheaper than a new 134300 at retail.

Dial colour: colourful dials are the fun part but usually cost more pre-owned. Black, blue and silver are the safe, easy-to-resell choices.

What to check before you buy

  • Box and papers: a Rolex warranty card that matches the serial on the watch is the single biggest resale factor.
  • Service history: ask when and where it was last serviced. A Rolex service record helps resale.
  • Ref check: listings mix 124300 and 134300, and the Chrono24 search pulls in other models. Confirm the ref, dial code and year before comparing prices.

Alternatives worth a look

  • Rolex Explorer (36 mm): Rolex's other simple three-hander, 36 mm with a 3-6-9 dial. (36 mm Oystersteel, Calibre 3230, retail $7,900 (124270))

  • Tissot PRX Powermatic 80: Integrated-bracelet three-hander at a tiny fraction of the price. (40 mm stainless steel, Powermatic 80, retail $850 (40 mm))

History

The Oyster Perpetual traces back to the Rolex Oyster, the waterproof case Rolex introduced in 1926, named after an oyster to highlight its water resistance.

In 1931 Rolex introduced the Oyster Perpetual with a self-winding movement using a 'Perpetual' rotor, and the Oyster Perpetual name still appears across the Rolex line.

In 2025 Rolex replaced the 41 mm ref. 124300 with the 134300, keeping the 41 mm Oystersteel case, 100 m water resistance and Calibre 3230 with a 70-hour power reserve, and adding a larger Twinlock crown.

Fun fact

Rolex sponsored swimmer Mercedes Gleitze, the first British woman to swim the English Channel, as one of its early ambassadors.

FAQ

How much is a Rolex Oyster Perpetual 41?

Rolex US retail for the current 134300-0001 is $7,050 (checked Oct 19, 2026). Jomashop listed a new 134300-0001 at $10,550 and the previous 124300 at $9,450.

What's the difference between the 124300 and 134300?

The 134300 replaced the 124300 in 2025. Both are 41 mm Oystersteel time-only watches with the Calibre 3230.

Does the Oyster Perpetual have a date?

No. The Oyster Perpetual 41 shows hours, minutes and seconds only.

Why is it called 'Oyster'?

Rolex's waterproof Oyster case dates to 1926. It was named after an oyster to highlight its water resistance.
