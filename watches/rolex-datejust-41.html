Which one I'd pick

126300 vs 126334: the 126300 has a smooth steel bezel and is $2,700 cheaper at retail. The 126334's white gold fluted bezel is the dressier, more traditional Datejust look. For an everyday watch, I'd take the 126300.

Oyster vs Jubilee: the Oyster bracelet is sportier; the Jubilee is the original Datejust bracelet. Pre-owned, check which one the listing actually includes.

What to check before you buy

  • Box and papers: a Rolex warranty card that matches the serial on the watch is the single biggest resale factor.
  • Service history: ask when and where it was last serviced. A Rolex service record helps resale.
  • Ref and dial: '126300' covers many dials. Match the full ref and dial before comparing prices, and avoid aftermarket dials or bezels.

Alternatives worth a look

  • Rolex Oyster Perpetual 41: Same 41 mm Rolex case without a date, cheaper at retail. (41 mm Oystersteel, Calibre 3230, retail $7,050 (134300-0001, Oystersteel))
  • Omega Seamaster Aqua Terra 150M: A dressy everyday Omega with a date, below retail grey-market. (41 mm stainless steel, Calibre 8900, retail $7,400 (220.10.41.21.01.002, 41 mm steel on steel))
  • Grand Seiko Heritage Collection 'Snowflake': Titanium Spring Drive with one of the best dials around. (41 mm high-intensity titanium, Calibre 9R65 Spring Drive, retail $6,900)

History

Rolex launched the Datejust in 1945. It was the first self-winding chronometer wristwatch to show the date in a window on the dial.

The 1945 watch used Rolex's waterproof Oyster case, first introduced in 1926, a fluted bezel and a new bracelet named for the occasion: the Jubilee.

Today the Datejust comes in sizes from 28 mm to 41 mm in steel, two-tone and solid gold. The Datejust 41 uses Rolex's Calibre 3235.

Fun fact

The Jubilee bracelet was designed specifically for the Datejust's 1945 debut.

FAQ

How much is a Rolex Datejust 41?

Rolex US retail is $8,950 for the 126300-0001 and $11,650 for the 126334-0001 with a white gold bezel (checked Oct 19, 2026). On Chrono24, the middle of the market for a 126300 was about $9,400 to $14,150.

What's the difference between the 126300 and 126334?

The 126300 has a smooth Oystersteel bezel. The 126334 has a fluted bezel in white gold and costs more.

When was the Datejust introduced?

In 1945, as the first self-winding chronometer wristwatch to show the date in a window on the dial.

Is the Datejust a good first Rolex?

It's the most classic everyday Rolex, and pre-owned asking prices start closer to retail than sports models like the Submariner or GMT-Master II.
