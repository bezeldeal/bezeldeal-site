History

In 1962 Jack Heuer was at the Sebring race and heard about the Carrera Panamericana, the dangerous Mexican road race. He liked the name.

Heuer launched the Carrera chronograph in 1963, designed for racing drivers with a clean, legible dial.

The modern 39 mm 'Glassbox' takes its nickname from the domed crystal, and the CBS2210 runs on TAG Heuer's in-house TH20-00 automatic chronograph.

Fun fact

The race was the Carrera Panamericana, a 1950s Mexican road race.
