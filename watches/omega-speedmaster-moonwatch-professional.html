Which one I'd pick

Hesalite (.001) vs sapphire (.002): the hesalite version is the Moon-era formula (Omega says it's inspired by the 4th-generation Speedmaster worn on the Moon) and $1,200 cheaper at retail. The sapphire version adds a scratch-resistant crystal. If you want 'the Moonwatch', I'd get the hesalite. If you hate scratches, get the sapphire.

What to check before you buy

  • Box and papers: an Omega warranty card dated and matched to the watch matters for resale. Grey-market new watches may carry the dealer's warranty instead of Omega's, so ask which one you're getting.
  • Hand-wound: there is no rotor, so a 'stopped' watch is usually just unwound. Check that the chronograph starts, stops and resets cleanly.
  • Fakes and red flags: the Speedmaster is heavily counterfeited. Be wary of prices far below the grey-market numbers above, stock photos only, or sellers who won't show the caseback and the card.

History

Omega launched the Speedmaster in 1957. The first model, the CK 2915 'Broad Arrow', was a 38.6 mm steel chronograph with a tachymeter scale on the bezel.

On March 1, 1965, NASA qualified the Speedmaster for all manned missions after a punishing test programme. Four years later Buzz Aldrin wore one on the lunar surface during Apollo 11, and the 'Moonwatch' name stuck. In 1970 a Speedmaster was used to time a critical manual engine burn on the crippled Apollo 13, and Omega later received NASA's Silver Snoopy Award.

In 2021 Omega moved the Moonwatch Professional to the Co-Axial Master Chronometer Calibre 3861, still manual-wind, with a 50-hour power reserve, and kept the asymmetric case, step dial and dot-over-90 bezel of the fourth-generation watch that went to the Moon.

Fun fact

During Apollo 13, Jack Swigert used his Speedmaster to time a critical 14-second engine burn for the return to Earth.

FAQ

How much is an Omega Speedmaster Moonwatch?

Omega's US retail is $7,800 for the hesalite 310.30.42.50.01.001 and $9,000 for the sapphire .002 on bracelet (checked Oct 19, 2026). New from grey-market dealers it was $6,650 at WatchMaxx, and pre-owned asking prices on Chrono24 mostly ran about $5,750 to $7,600.

What's the difference between hesalite and sapphire Speedmasters?

The .001 has a hesalite (acrylic) crystal, the classic Moonwatch look. The .002 has a scratch-resistant sapphire crystal and costs $1,200 more at retail.

Is the Speedmaster Moonwatch automatic?

No. The Moonwatch Professional uses the manual-winding Calibre 3861 with about 50 hours of power reserve.

Is the Speedmaster water resistant?

It's rated to 50 m (5 bar). Fine for rain and hand-washing, but it isn't a dive watch.

Which Speedmaster went to the Moon?

Buzz Aldrin wore a Speedmaster during Apollo 11 in 1969. NASA had qualified the Speedmaster for all manned missions in 1965.
