What to check before you buy

  • Box and papers: a brand warranty card matters for resale. Grey-market new watches may carry the dealer's warranty instead of the brand's, so ask which one you're getting.
  • GMT function: check that the local hour hand jumps in one-hour steps and the date follows it.
  • Fakes and red flags: avoid prices far below the grey-market numbers above, stock photos only, or sellers who won't show the caseback, clasp and warranty card.

History

Longines was founded in 1832 and is based in Saint-Imier, Switzerland. In 1931 it worked with Charles Lindbergh on the Hour Angle aviation watch.

In 1925 Longines made its first dual-time-zone wristwatch, with the Zulu flag on the dial. 'Zulu' is aviation shorthand for the letter Z, which designates universal time.

The Spirit Zulu Time arrived in 2022. The 39 mm L3.802.4.63.6 uses the COSC-certified L844.4 with a silicon balance spring and up to 72 hours of power reserve.

Fun fact

'Zulu' is aviation shorthand for UTC; Longines first used the Zulu Time name in 1925.

FAQ

How much is a Longines Spirit Zulu Time?

Longines' US site lists the 39 mm L3.802.4.63.6 at $3,550 (checked Oct 19, 2026). It was $2,662.50 new at Jomashop and $2,695 at WatchMaxx.

What does Zulu Time mean?

Zulu refers to the letter Z, which designates universal time for aviators. Longines first used it on a 1925 dual-time watch.

Is the Spirit Zulu Time a true GMT?

Yes. Longines calls it a GMT with a 24-hour hand and time-zone function, and its L844.4 is COSC-certified.

What size is it?

This ref is 39 mm and 13.5 mm thick; Longines also makes a 42 mm version.
