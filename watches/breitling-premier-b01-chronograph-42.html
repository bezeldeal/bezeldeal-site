What to check before you buy

  • Exact reference: dial colour, size and strap change the price. Match the full ref (AB0145221B1A1) before comparing deals.

History

Breitling was founded by Léon Breitling in Saint-Imier in 1884, and is best known for aviation (Navitimer) and diving (Superocean) watches.

Breitling developed the B01 self-winding column-wheel chronograph movement in 2009.

The Premier B01 Chronograph 42 AB0145221B1A1 checked here has a black dial, per WatchMaxx and Jomashop.
