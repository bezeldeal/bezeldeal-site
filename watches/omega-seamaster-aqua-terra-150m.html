History

The Seamaster name goes back to 1948, when Omega released a water-resistant dress-style watch, conceived as a robust everyday watch for 'town, sea, and country'.

Omega introduced the Seamaster Aqua Terra in 2002 as a watch for both land and water, the everyday counterpart to the dive models. The 41 mm .001 had a black dial with a horizontal 'teak' pattern inspired by the wooden decks of sailboats.

The current 41 mm .002 swaps the teak for a glossy lacquered black dial and keeps the Co-Axial Master Chronometer Calibre 8900 with a 60-hour power reserve and 150 m water resistance.

FAQ

How much is an Omega Aqua Terra 41 mm?

Omega's US site lists the current 220.10.41.21.01.002 on bracelet at $7,400 (checked Oct 19, 2026). The previous teak-dial .001 was $5,680 new at Jomashop.

Is the 220.10.41.21.01.001 discontinued?

Omega's US product page for it says 'Sorry, but this watch is no longer available.' Grey-market dealers still had new stock on Oct 19, 2026.

What movement is in the Aqua Terra?

The Omega Co-Axial Master Chronometer Calibre 8900, automatic, with a 60-hour power reserve.

Is the Aqua Terra a dive watch?

No. It's rated to 150 m, but it has no dive bezel. It's Omega's everyday Seamaster.
