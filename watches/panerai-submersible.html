What to check before you buy

  • Warranty: grey-market new watches usually carry the dealer's warranty, not the brand's. Ask which one you get and whether the brand's warranty card is included.
  • Exact reference: dial colour, size and strap change the price. Match the full ref (PAM01590) before comparing deals.
  • Fakes and red flags: be wary of prices far below the ranges above, stock photos only, or sellers who won't show the caseback, serial and papers.

History

Giovanni Panerai opened his first watch shop in Florence in 1860, and Panerai became an official supplier of precision instruments to the Royal Italian Navy.

The Submersible PAM01590 checked here is a 42 mm steel diver with a white dial, the Panerai Calibre P.900 (72-hour power reserve) and 300 m water resistance, per Jomashop.
