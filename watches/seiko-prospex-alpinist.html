History

Seiko launched the Laurel Alpinist in 1959, as mountaineering and skiing took off in Japan. Seiko calls it its first sports watch.

The SPB121 was a modern Prospex take on the idea: a 39.5 mm case with a green dial and an internal rotating compass bezel. Seiko no longer lists it on its US site.

Seiko's current US Alpinist line lists the SPB503, SPB505 and SPB507. The SPB507 uses the 6R55 automatic with about 72 hours of power reserve and a sapphire crystal with a date magnifier.
