History

Rolex introduced the Day-Date at the Swiss Industries Fair in Basel in 1956. It was the first watch to show both the date and the day of the week spelled in full. Lyndon B. Johnson became the first US president to wear a gold Day-Date.

The current Day-Date 40 in 18 kt yellow gold, ref. 228238, is 40 mm with a fluted bezel and President bracelet, per Rolex.
