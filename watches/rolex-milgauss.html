History

Rolex introduced the Milgauss in 1956 (ref. 6541), brought it back in 2007 as ref. 116400 after nearly 20 years, and discontinued it in 2023.

The 116400GV checked here is a 40 mm steel watch with a green sapphire crystal (the 'GV'), Rolex Calibre 3131 and a 48-hour power reserve, per Jomashop.
