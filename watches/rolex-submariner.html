Which one I'd pick

124060 vs 126610LN: the no-date 124060 is the purist's pick: clean dial with no date, $1,300 cheaper at retail and cheaper pre-owned. The 126610LN adds a date, if you want one.

New vs pre-owned: new Rolex watches are sold only through Official Rolex Jewelers, so you buy at retail when one is available to you. Pre-owned is available right now but, on today's numbers, costs more than retail.

What to check before you buy

  • Box and papers: a Rolex warranty card that matches the serial on the watch is the single biggest resale factor.
  • Service history: ask when and where it was serviced. A Rolex service record helps resale.
  • Fakes and red flags: the Submariner is the most counterfeited watch there is. A Sub listed well below retail is the biggest red flag. Buy from established dealers and have it checked by a watchmaker.

Alternatives worth a look

  • Tudor Black Bay 58: Rolex's sister brand: vintage-style 39 mm diver, often below retail. (39 mm stainless steel, Calibre MT5400-U, retail From $4,975 (rubber); $5,225–$5,350 on bracelet)
  • Omega Seamaster Diver 300M: Ceramic diver with a Master Chronometer movement, under retail grey-market. (42 mm stainless steel, Calibre 8800, retail $6,700 (steel bracelet; blue .03.001 or black .01.001))
  • Tudor Pelagos 39: Titanium tool diver from Rolex's sister brand. (39 mm titanium, Calibre MT5400, retail $5,625 (25407N))

History

Rolex introduced the Submariner in 1953 as its first divers' wristwatch, waterproof to 100 m at launch. It set the template for the modern dive watch: rotating timing bezel, big luminous markers, screw-down crown.

Pop culture helped. Sean Connery wore a Submariner ref. 6538 as James Bond in Dr. No (1962), long before Omega took over the role.

In 2020 Rolex updated the line to a 41 mm case and the new-generation Calibres 3230 (no-date, ref. 124060) and 3235 (date, ref. 126610LN), both with a 70-hour power reserve.

FAQ

How much is a Rolex Submariner in 2026?

Rolex US retail is $10,050 for the no-date 124060 and $11,350 for the 126610LN Submariner Date (checked Oct 19, 2026). On Chrono24, the middle of the market was asking about $12,450 to $15,000 for a 124060 and $13,800 to $16,500 for a 126610LN.

Why is the Submariner more expensive pre-owned than new?

New Submariners are sold only through Official Rolex Jewelers, so supply at retail is limited. On Oct 19, 2026, the middle of Chrono24's asking prices was above retail for both the 124060 and 126610LN.

What's the difference between the 124060 and 126610LN?

The 124060 has no date and uses Calibre 3230. The 126610LN has a date and uses Calibre 3235. Both are 41 mm, 300 m Oystersteel divers with a 70-hour power reserve.

Is the Rolex Submariner waterproof?

Yes. The current Submariner is rated to 300 m. The original 1953 model was rated to 100 m.
