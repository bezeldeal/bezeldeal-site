History

Grand Seiko began in December 1960 as Seiko's highest-grade domestic line, and adopted Spring Drive in 2004 with the Calibre 9R65.

SBGW231 is a 37.3 mm steel dress watch with the hand-wound Calibre 9S64 and a three-day power reserve, per Grand Seiko's US boutique.
