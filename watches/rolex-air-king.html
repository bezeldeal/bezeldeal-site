History

Introduced in 1945, the Air-King was part of Rolex's post-war aviation-themed watches and is the only model from that series still in production. Rolex re-introduced it in 2018, with a dial inspired by the counter Rolex built for the Bloodhound SSC land-speed car.

The current Air-King, ref. 126900, is a 40 mm Oystersteel watch, per Rolex.
