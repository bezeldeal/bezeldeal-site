What to check before you buy

  • Warranty: grey-market new watches usually carry the dealer's warranty, not the brand's. Ask which one you get and whether the brand's warranty card is included.
  • Exact reference: dial colour, size and strap change the price. Match the full ref (M79360N-0001) before comparing deals.
  • Fakes and red flags: be wary of prices far below the ranges above, stock photos only, or sellers who won't show the caseback, serial and papers.

Alternatives worth a look

History

Tudor says the name 'The Tudor' was first registered as a brand in 1926 on behalf of Rolex founder Hans Wilsdorf.

The Black Bay Chrono m79360n-0001 is a 41 mm steel chronograph with the Tudor Calibre MT5813, based on the Breitling B01, with 70 hours of power reserve and 200 m water resistance, per Jomashop.
