History

NOMOS Glashütte is a German watchmaker based in Glashütte, Saxony, founded by Roland Schwertner in January 1990, two months after the fall of the Berlin Wall.

NOMOS says the Tangente 38 ref. 164 measures 37.5 mm and 6.8 mm high and runs on its hand-wound DUW 4001 calibre for up to 53 hours.
