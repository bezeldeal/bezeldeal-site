What to check before you buy

History

Oris was founded in 1904 in Hölstein, Switzerland, where it's still based.

In 2020 Oris introduced the Calibre 400, its in-house automatic with a five-day power reserve and high resistance to magnetism.

The Aquis Date Calibre 400 was the first watch to carry it. Oris backs Calibre 400 watches with a 10-year warranty when you register with MyOris, and quotes 10-year service intervals.
