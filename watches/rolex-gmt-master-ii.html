Which one I'd pick

BLNR vs GRNR: same price, same movement. The BLNR 'Batman' is the classic look. The GRNR's left-side crown is the oddball, which you'll either love or find awkward, so try one on first.

New vs pre-owned: at retail it's $11,800 if an Official Rolex Jeweler will sell you one. Pre-owned is available today, but the middle of the market asks roughly 50% to 80% over retail.

What to check before you buy

  • Box and papers: a Rolex warranty card that matches the serial on the watch is the single biggest resale factor.
  • Service history: ask when and where it was last serviced. A Rolex service record helps resale.
  • Fakes and red flags: GMTs are heavily faked. A 'Batman' listed near or under retail is the biggest red flag. Buy from an established dealer and have a watchmaker check the movement.

Alternatives worth a look

  • Tudor Black Bay Pro: Tudor's 39 mm GMT with a fixed 24-hour bezel, below retail grey-market. (39 mm stainless steel, Calibre MT5652, retail $5,075 (M79470-0001))
  • Longines Spirit Zulu Time: A true GMT with a ceramic bezel for a fraction of the price. (39 mm stainless steel, Calibre L844.4, retail $3,550 (L3.802.4.63.6))
  • Rolex Explorer (36 mm): A simpler Rolex tool watch, closer to retail on the secondary market. (36 mm Oystersteel, Calibre 3230, retail $7,900 (124270))

History

Rolex launched the GMT-Master in the mid-1950s, developed with Pan Am for its pilots and navigators. Rolex dates it to 1955; Wikipedia's article says 1954. The 24-hour hand and two-tone bezel let a crew read a second time zone at a glance.

The GMT-Master II line moved to ceramic bezels. From 2013 to 2019 Rolex made the steel 116710BLNR with a blue-and-black Cerachrom bezel, nicknamed 'Batman', and in 2018 it brought back the red-and-blue 'Pepsi' on a new steel model.

In 2022 Rolex added a green-and-black bezel version with the crown and date on the left side of the case. Today's steel models run on the Calibre 3285.

Fun fact

The red-and-blue bezel became known as "Pepsi"; the blue-and-black version introduced in 2013 is nicknamed "Batman".

FAQ

How much is a Rolex GMT-Master II in 2026?

Rolex US retail is $11,800 for the 126710BLNR-0003 and 126710GRNR-0004 on the Oyster bracelet (checked Oct 19, 2026). On Chrono24, the middle of the market was asking about $17,500 to $21,600 for a 126710BLNR.

Why is the 'Batman' called Batman?

Collectors nicknamed the blue-and-black Cerachrom bezel 'Batman'. The steel 116710BLNR with that bezel was made from 2013 to 2019, and the current ref is 126710BLNR.

Which GMT-Master II has the crown on the left?

The 126710GRNR, launched in 2022 with a green-and-black bezel, has its crown and date on the left side of the case.

Is it worth buying a GMT-Master II pre-owned?

Only if you accept paying well above retail. On Oct 19, 2026, Chrono24's middle-of-market asks for a 126710BLNR ran $17,500 to $21,600 against $11,800 retail.
