What to check before you buy

  • Box and papers: a Breitling warranty card matched to the watch, plus the COSC certificate, help resale. Grey-market new may carry a dealer warranty instead of Breitling's.
  • Chronograph function: check start, stop and reset, and that the date changes properly.
  • Fakes and red flags: Breitling chronographs are commonly faked. Be wary of prices far under the pre-owned range and sellers without caseback and card photos.

History

The Chronomat was introduced in 1984, when ultra-thin quartz watches were the fashion. Breitling bet on a big mechanical chronograph instead, based on the Frecce Tricolori chronograph developed in 1983 with Italy's aerobatic team.

Its rider tabs on the bezel became a Breitling signature. In 2020 Breitling relaunched the line as the Chronomat B01 42 and brought back the 'Rouleaux' bracelet from the 1984 original.

Today's steel Chronomat uses the in-house Breitling Manufacture Calibre 01, a COSC-certified automatic chronograph with about 70 hours of power reserve, in a 42 mm case water-resistant to 200 m.

FAQ

How much is the Breitling Chronomat B01 42?

Breitling's US retail for the steel AB0134101B1A1 is $9,550 (checked Oct 19, 2026). New it was $7,350 at WatchMaxx and Jomashop, and Chrono24 asking prices mostly ran about $5,550 to $8,700.

How thick is the Chronomat B01 42?

Breitling lists it at 15.1 mm thick and 42 mm wide, with a 22 mm lug width.

What movement is in the Chronomat B01 42?

The Breitling Manufacture Calibre 01: in-house, self-winding, 28,800 vph, 47 jewels, about 70 hours of power reserve.

Is the Chronomat water resistant?

Yes, to 200 m (20 bar), with a screw-locked crown.

What are the tabs on the Chronomat bezel?

They're called rider tabs, part of the Chronomat design since the 1984 original.
