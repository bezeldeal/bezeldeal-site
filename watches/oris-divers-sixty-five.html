History

Oris was founded in 1904 in Hölstein, Switzerland, by Paul Cattin and Georges Christian.

Oris recreated its original diver in 2015 as the Divers Sixty-Five, a vintage-inspired collection.

The 01 733 7771 3154 checked here is a 38 mm automatic with the Oris Calibre 733 (base SW 200-1), per Jomashop.
