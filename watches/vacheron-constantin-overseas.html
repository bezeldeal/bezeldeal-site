What to check before you buy

  • Straps and tools: a full set should include the bracelet, the extra straps and the papers. Missing straps are expensive to replace.
  • Box and papers: the warranty card matters for resale.
  • Fakes and red flags: buy from an established dealer and get it authenticated at this price.

Alternatives worth a look

  • Audemars Piguet Royal Oak Selfwinding: The original integrated-bracelet icon, at a bigger premium. (41 mm stainless steel (15510ST), Calibre 4302, retail $31,900 (15510ST.OO.1320ST.06, AP US site))

History

Vacheron Constantin has been making watches since 1755. Since 1880 it has used the Maltese cross as its symbol, inspired by a component of the barrel.

The Overseas launched in 1996 as the maison's luxury sports and travel watch, building on the design language of the 1977 ref. 222.

The current 4520V/210A-B128 is 41 mm and 10.69 mm thick, with a Maltese-cross-inspired bezel, 150 m water resistance and a quick-change system for its steel bracelet, leather and rubber straps.

Fun fact

Vacheron Constantin, founded in 1755, claims the longest uninterrupted watchmaking history.
