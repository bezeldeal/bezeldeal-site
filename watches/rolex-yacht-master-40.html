History

The Yacht-Master was first introduced in 1992 as reference 16628 in 18-karat yellow gold.

Today's Yacht-Master 40 ref. 126622 combines an Oystersteel case and bracelet with a 950 platinum bezel and dial, and Rolex describes its bidirectional 60-minute bezel as made entirely from precious metal or fitted with Cerachrom.
