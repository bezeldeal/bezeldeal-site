What to check before you buy

  • Warranty: grey-market new watches usually carry the dealer's warranty, not the brand's. Ask which one you get and whether the brand's warranty card is included.
  • Exact reference: dial colour, size and strap change the price. Match the full ref (H70605731) before comparing deals.
  • Returns: check the store's return window and whether opening the box or removing stickers affects it.

History

Hamilton was founded in 1892 in Lancaster, Pennsylvania, and moved manufacturing to Switzerland after 1969.

Hamilton watches have appeared in many films, including Interstellar (2014).

The Khaki Field Murph H70605731 has a black dial and an automatic movement, per WatchMaxx and Jomashop.
