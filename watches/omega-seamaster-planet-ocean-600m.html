What to check before you buy

  • Box and papers: a brand warranty card matters for resale. Grey-market new watches may carry the dealer's warranty instead of the brand's, so ask which one you're getting.
  • Size: at 43.5 mm with a helium valve this is a big watch. Try one on before you buy.
  • Fakes and red flags: avoid prices far below the grey-market numbers above, stock photos only, or sellers who won't show the caseback, clasp and warranty card.

History

The Seamaster line dates to 1948. The Planet Ocean joined it in 2005 as Omega's more heavy-duty professional dive watch, borrowing from Omega's dive-watch past.

In 2019, three experimental Seamaster Planet Ocean Ultra Deep Professional watches survived a 10,928 m dive to the bottom of the Mariana Trench, attached to a submersible.

The 215.30.44.21.01.001 is the 43.5 mm steel model with the Co-Axial Master Chronometer Calibre 8900 and a 60-hour power reserve. Omega has since replaced it with newer Planet Ocean models.
