History

Oris was founded in 1904 in Hölstein, Switzerland, by Paul Cattin and Georges Christian.

Oris introduced the in-house Calibre 400 in 2020: antimagnetic to 2,250 gauss, a 5-day (120-hour) power reserve, a recommended 10-year service interval and a 10-year warranty.
