History

Oris was founded in 1904 in Hölstein, Switzerland, by Paul Cattin and Georges Christian.

Oris introduced its signature Big Crown pilot's watch in 1938, with an oversized crown that a pilot could operate with gloves on and large Arabic numerals for quick reading in the cockpit.

The 01 754 7798 4069 checked here is a 40 mm steel version with the Oris Calibre 754 (base SW 200-1), per Jomashop.
