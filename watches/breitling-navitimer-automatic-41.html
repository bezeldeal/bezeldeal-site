History

Breitling was founded by Léon Breitling in Saint-Imier in 1884, and is best known for aviation (Navitimer) and diving (Superocean) watches.

In the 1940s Breitling added a circular slide rule to the Chronomat bezel; the Navitimer, first made with AOPA logos, later carried the winged-B logo from 1956.

The Navitimer Automatic 41 A17329161C1A1 has a blue dial and an automatic movement, per WatchMaxx and Jomashop.
