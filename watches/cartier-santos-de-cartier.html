What to check before you buy

  • Box and papers: a brand warranty card matters for resale. Grey-market new watches may carry the dealer's warranty instead of the brand's, so ask which one you're getting.
  • Bracelet: make sure all links and the original clasp are included; Cartier links are expensive to replace.
  • Fakes and red flags: avoid prices far below the grey-market numbers above, stock photos only, or sellers who won't show the caseback, clasp and warranty card.

History

In 1904, the Brazilian aviator Alberto Santos-Dumont complained to his friend Louis Cartier that pocket watches were unreliable and impractical to use while flying. Cartier's answer was a wristwatch.

Cartier went on to sell the Santos commercially; Wikipedia dates the line's creation to 1911.

Today's Santos de Cartier keeps the square case and exposed bezel screws. The medium WSSA0029 in steel is 8.82 mm thick and water resistant to about 100 m, per Cartier.

Fun fact

It is often described as the first pilot's watch.
