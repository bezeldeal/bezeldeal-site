History

In 1952 Willy Breitling developed a wrist chronograph with a circular slide rule that let pilots do flight calculations on the bezel.

Two years later the Aircraft Owners and Pilots Association (AOPA) made the design its official timepiece. The 'navigation timer' became the Navitimer.

Breitling was founded in 1884 in Saint-Imier by Léon Breitling and is based in Grenchen today. The current Navitimer B01 Chronograph 43 uses the in-house Breitling 01 with about 70 hours of power reserve.
