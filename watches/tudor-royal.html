What to check before you buy

  • Warranty: grey-market new watches usually carry the dealer's warranty, not the brand's. Ask which one you get and whether the brand's warranty card is included.
  • Exact reference: dial colour, size and strap change the price. Match the full ref (M28600-0005) before comparing deals.
  • Fakes and red flags: be wary of prices far below the ranges above, stock photos only, or sellers who won't show the caseback, serial and papers.

Alternatives worth a look

  • Tudor Black Bay 54: Tudor's sport-watch alternative. (37 mm steel, Manufacture Calibre MT5400, retail $4,725 (m79000n-0001, per Tudor))
  • Tissot PRX Powermatic 80: Cheaper integrated-bracelet automatic. (40 mm stainless steel, Powermatic 80, retail $850 (40 mm))
  • Tudor 1926: Tudor's dress watch. (41 mm steel, Calibre T601, retail $2,650 (m91650-0001, per Tudor))

History

Tudor says the name 'The Tudor' was first registered as a brand in 1926 on behalf of Rolex founder Hans Wilsdorf.
