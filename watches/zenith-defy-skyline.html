What to check before you buy

  • Warranty: grey-market new watches usually carry the dealer's warranty, not the brand's. Ask which one you get and whether the brand's warranty card is included.
  • Exact reference: dial colour, size and strap change the price. Match the full ref (03.9300.3620/51.I001) before comparing deals.
  • Fakes and red flags: be wary of prices far below the ranges above, stock photos only, or sellers who won't show the caseback, serial and papers.

History

Zenith was founded by Georges Favre-Jacot in Le Locle in 1865; its El Primero, unveiled on January 10, 1969, was the first high-frequency automatic chronograph movement.

The Defy Skyline 03.9300.3620/51.I001 checked here is an automatic, per Jomashop and WatchMaxx.
