Which one I'd pick

Moonwatch or '57? The Moonwatch is the cheaper, history-first choice. The '57 adds a co-axial 9906 movement with a display back and a date, and has the 1957-style straight lugs. Go '57 if you want the vintage look with modern internals.

History

Omega introduced the Speedmaster in 1957 as a sport and racing chronograph, the same year it launched the Railmaster and the Seamaster 300.

Today's Speedmaster '57 (332.10.41.51.01.001) is a 40.5 mm steel chronograph with the hand-wound Omega Calibre 9906, a 60-hour power reserve and a transparent caseback, per the Jomashop listing.
