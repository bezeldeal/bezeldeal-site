What to check before you buy

  • Box and papers: a Rolex warranty card that matches the serial on the watch is the single biggest resale factor.
  • Service history: ask when and where it was last serviced. A Rolex service record helps resale.
  • Bezel and valve: check the Cerachrom insert for chips and ask about the last pressure test, especially on a watch that's been dived.

Alternatives worth a look

  • Rolex Submariner: The smaller, more famous Rolex diver. (41 mm Oystersteel, Calibre 3230 (no-date) / 3235 (date), retail $10,050 (124060); $11,350 (126610LN))
  • Omega Seamaster Planet Ocean 600M: Omega's 600 m diver, below retail grey-market. (43.5 mm stainless steel, Calibre 8900, retail Discontinued ref.; current Planet Ocean models $8,600–$9,200 on Omega US)
  • Tudor Pelagos 39: Titanium tool diver from Rolex's sister brand. (39 mm titanium, Calibre MT5400, retail $5,625 (25407N))

History

Rolex launched the Sea-Dweller to the public in 1967 with a depth rating of 610 m and a gas escape valve, developed for saturation diving, that lets helium out of the case during decompression.

Later the 16660 'Triple-Six' raised the rating to 1,220 m. The Sea-Dweller has been standard issue for Comex divers since 1977.

Until the ref. 126600 arrived in 2017, Sea-Dwellers had no Cyclops lens over the date. The current 43 mm model added one.

FAQ

How much is a Rolex Sea-Dweller?

Rolex US retail for the 126600-0002 is $14,550 (checked Oct 19, 2026). On Chrono24, the middle of the market was asking about $11,900 to $16,000, with a median of about $13,700.

What's the difference between the Sea-Dweller and the Submariner?

The Sea-Dweller is 43 mm, rated to 1,220 m and has a helium escape valve for saturation diving. The Submariner is 41 mm and rated to 300 m.

Does the Sea-Dweller have a Cyclops?

The current 126600 does. Sea-Dwellers before the 126600 (2017) didn't have one.

What is the helium escape valve for?

In saturation diving, helium gets into the watch. The valve lets it escape during decompression so the crystal doesn't pop off.
