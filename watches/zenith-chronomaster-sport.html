History

Zenith unveiled the El Primero on January 10, 1969. It was the first high-frequency automatic chronograph movement.

When the brand's owners later abandoned mechanical chronographs, watchmaker Charles Vermot preserved the El Primero, which let Zenith bring it back.

Zenith launched the Chronomaster Sport in January 2021 with the El Primero 3600, running at 36,000 vibrations an hour so the chronograph can measure tenths of a second, with about 60 hours of power reserve.

Fun fact

Zenith employee Charles Vermot secretly kept El Primero tooling and plans after production stopped in 1975, enabling its 1984 revival.
