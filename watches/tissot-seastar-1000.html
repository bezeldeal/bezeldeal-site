History

Tissot was founded in Le Locle, Switzerland, in 1853 by Charles-Félicien Tissot and his son Charles-Émile.

The Seastar 1000 Powermatic 80 T120.407.11.041.03 is a 43 mm, 12.7 mm thick diver with the Powermatic 80.111 (80-hour power reserve) and 300 m water resistance, per Jomashop.
