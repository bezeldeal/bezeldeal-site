History

Tudor says the name 'The Tudor' was first registered as a brand in 1926 on behalf of Rolex founder Hans Wilsdorf.

The current Ranger, m79950-0001, has a 39 mm steel case, domed sapphire crystal, the COSC-certified Manufacture Calibre MT5402 with about 70 hours of power reserve, and 100 m water resistance, per Tudor's US site.
