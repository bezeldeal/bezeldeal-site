History

Tissot was founded in 1853 in Le Locle, Switzerland, where it's still headquartered.

The original PRX was a 1978 quartz watch with a slim barrel-shaped case and an integrated bracelet. 'PR' stands for Precise and Robust, and the 'X' for 10 bar of water resistance.

Tissot revived the PRX in 2021, first in quartz and then as the automatic PRX Powermatic 80, with an 80-hour power reserve and a Nivachron balance spring.

Fun fact

PRX stands for Precise and Robust; the X refers to 10 bar water resistance.
