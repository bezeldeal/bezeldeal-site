What to check before you buy

  • Box and papers: a brand warranty card matters for resale. Grey-market new watches may carry the dealer's warranty instead of the brand's, so ask which one you're getting.
  • Titanium scratches: grade 2 titanium is light but marks easily. Check the bracelet and case in good light.
  • Fakes and red flags: avoid prices far below the grey-market numbers above, stock photos only, or sellers who won't show the caseback, clasp and warranty card.

Alternatives worth a look

History

Tudor introduced the Pelagos in 2012 as its technical titanium dive watch.

The smaller Pelagos 39 arrived in 2022: 39 mm of grade 2 titanium, 200 m water resistance and no helium valve, aimed at everyday wear as much as diving.

It runs on the Manufacture Calibre MT5400, COSC-certified, with a power reserve of about 70 hours. Tudor says it regulates the assembled watch to -2/+4 seconds a day, tighter than COSC's -4/+6.
