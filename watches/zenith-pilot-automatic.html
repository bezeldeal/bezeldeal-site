History

Zenith was founded by Georges Favre-Jacot in Le Locle in 1865; its El Primero, unveiled on January 10, 1969, was the first high-frequency automatic chronograph movement.

The Pilot Automatic 03.4000.3620/21.I001 has a black dial, per Jomashop and WatchMaxx.
