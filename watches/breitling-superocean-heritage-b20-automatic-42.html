History

Breitling's original Superocean appeared in 1957 as a 200 m dive watch, in a time-only ref. 1004 and a chronograph ref. 807.

The Superocean Heritage brought that vintage design back in 2007 for the line's 50th anniversary.

The current Heritage B20 Automatic 42 is 14.35 mm thick, rated to 200 m and powered by the Breitling B20 manufacture movement with about 70 hours of power reserve.
