What to check before you buy

  • Warranty: grey-market new watches usually carry the dealer's warranty, not the brand's. Ask which one you get and whether the brand's warranty card is included.
  • Exact reference: dial colour, size and strap change the price. Match the full ref (WAZ1110.BA0875) before comparing deals.
  • Fakes and red flags: be wary of prices far below the ranges above, stock photos only, or sellers who won't show the caseback, serial and papers.

History

Heuer was founded by Edouard Heuer in St-Imier in 1860; TAG Heuer's current lines include Formula 1, Aquaracer, Carrera and Monaco.

The Formula 1 WAZ1110.BA0875 is a black-dial quartz watch on a steel bracelet, per WatchMaxx and Jomashop.
