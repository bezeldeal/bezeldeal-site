History

Grand Seiko began in December 1960 as Seiko's highest-grade domestic line, and adopted Spring Drive in 2004 with the Calibre 9R65.

Grand Seiko introduced the redesigned 9SA5 mechanical calibre in 2020. It powers the SLGH005, which won the GPHG Men's Watch Prize in 2021.
