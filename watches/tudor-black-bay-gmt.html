What to check before you buy

  • Warranty: grey-market new watches usually carry the dealer's warranty, not the brand's. Ask which one you get and whether the brand's warranty card is included.
  • Exact reference: dial colour, size and strap change the price. Match the full ref (M79830RB-0001) before comparing deals.
  • Fakes and red flags: be wary of prices far below the ranges above, stock photos only, or sellers who won't show the caseback, serial and papers.

Alternatives worth a look

  • Rolex GMT-Master II: The Pepsi icon, far more expensive. (40 mm Oystersteel, Calibre 3285, retail $11,800 (126710BLNR-0003 and 126710GRNR-0004, Oyster bracelet))
  • Tudor Black Bay 58 GMT: Smaller 39 mm GMT. (39 mm Stainless Steel, Tudor Calibre MT5450-U, retail $5,525 (list price per Jomashop & WatchMaxx))
  • Longines Spirit Zulu Time: A Swiss GMT for less. (39 mm stainless steel, Calibre L844.4, retail $3,550 (L3.802.4.63.6))

History

Tudor says the name 'The Tudor' was first registered as a brand in 1926 on behalf of Rolex founder Hans Wilsdorf.

The Black Bay GMT m79830rb-0001 has a 41 mm steel case, a bidirectional 48-notch bezel with 24-hour graduations in matte blue and burgundy, Manufacture Calibre MT5652 with about 70 hours of power reserve, and 200 m water resistance, per Tudor.
