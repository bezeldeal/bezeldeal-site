What to check before you buy

  • Warranty: grey-market new watches usually carry the dealer's warranty, not the brand's. Ask which one you get and whether the brand's warranty card is included.
  • Exact reference: dial colour, size and strap change the price. Match the full ref (M79000N-0001) before comparing deals.

History

Tudor says the name 'The Tudor' was first registered as a brand in 1926 on behalf of Rolex founder Hans Wilsdorf.

Tudor calls the Black Bay 54 perhaps the most true-to-form Black Bay yet: 37 mm steel, domed sapphire crystal, Manufacture Calibre MT5400 with about 70 hours of power reserve, and 200 m water resistance.
