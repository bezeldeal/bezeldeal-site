What to check before you buy

  • Box and papers: a brand warranty card matters for resale. Grey-market new watches may carry the dealer's warranty instead of the brand's, so ask which one you're getting.
  • Thickness: the Black Bay Pro is a chunky 39 mm watch. Try it on if you can.
  • Fakes and red flags: avoid prices far below the grey-market numbers above, stock photos only, or sellers who won't show the caseback, clasp and warranty card.

History

Montres Tudor SA has made Tudor watches since 1946, and the Black Bay line, launched in 2012, is built around the brand's dive-watch past.

Tudor launched the Black Bay Pro in 2022 at Watches & Wonders: a 39 mm steel watch with a fixed 24-hour bezel and a GMT function.

It uses Tudor's Manufacture Calibre MT5652, COSC-certified, with a power reserve of about 70 hours and 200 m water resistance.
