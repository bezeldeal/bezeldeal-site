What to check before you buy

  • Crystal: the box-shaped Hardlex crystal is part of the look but can scratch. Check it under light.
  • Water resistance: 5 bar. Fine for rain and hand-washing, not for swimming.
  • Ref check: Cocktail Time comes in many dial colours. Match the ref (SRPB43) before buying.

Alternatives worth a look

  • Grand Seiko Heritage Collection 'Snowflake': Where the textured-dial idea goes at the top end. (41 mm high-intensity titanium, Calibre 9R65 Spring Drive, retail $6,900)
  • Tissot PRX Powermatic 80: Swiss automatic with an 80-hour reserve, below retail. (40 mm stainless steel, Powermatic 80, retail $850 (40 mm))
  • Hamilton Khaki Field Mechanical: Another under-$700 mechanical classic. (38 mm stainless steel, Calibre H-50, retail $675 (38 mm))

History

Cocktail Time began in 2010 as a Japan-only collaboration with Shinobu Ishigaki, founder of Bar Ishinohana in Shibuya, Tokyo, whose cocktails inspired the dials.

The series later became part of Seiko's Presage line.

The SRPB43 has a blue sunburst dial, a 40.5 mm case and Seiko's 4R35 automatic with about 41 hours of power reserve.
